Sparx Media Project Media Files
===============================

Add your Sparx Media screenshots here:

1.png - Homepage hero / first fold
2.png - Services or capabilities section
3.png - Portfolio / case highlights

Recommended specs:
- PNG or JPG, 16:10 or similar aspect ratio
- ~1920x1200 (optimize < 500KB)

Once images are added, they will show in the gallery and grid.
